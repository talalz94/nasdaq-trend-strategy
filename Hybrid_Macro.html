03

What worked, what didn't — and why

WORKED
  • Bonds as the off-leg. When out of Nasdaq, hold long treasuries (TLT) not 0% cash — they rally in flight-to-safety (2008, 2000–02). +2–3%/yr through bad decades.
  • De-lever in chop. When the trend is weak (low efficiency ratio), hold 1× QQQ instead of 3× TQQQ. Cuts max drawdown −67% → −47% — leverage decay in chop is pure poison.
  • Yield-curve overlay ★. Go defensive when 10y−3mo inverts. It leads recessions by ~a year, so it steps aside before the crash. Best dead-decade result: +8.4%/yr, −39% DD.
DIDN'T WORK
  • VIX overlay. VIX spikes at the bottom — it sells you out after the crash and buys back high. Lagging, whipsaws.
  • Dollar overlay. Too many false risk-off signals; bled returns in every era.
  • Mean-reversion in chop. Buying dips in chop underperformed simply de-levering — more complexity, worse result.
  • Smaller/faster EMA in chop. Backfired badly (−4%/yr, −78% DD in 2000–09) — a faster line whipsaws more, not less.
  • Selective SQQQ. Even restricted to strong downtrends, shorting via SQQQ lost money and deepened drawdowns. Cash/bonds beat it.
The macro lesson: leading beats coincident

The yield curve works because it's a leading indicator — it inverts ~12–18 months before recessions, so you de-risk early. VIX and the dollar fail because they're coincident/lagging — they scream danger exactly when the damage is already done, guaranteeing you sell the bottom. For adapting a strategy, only leading signals add value.

05

Recommended recipe & caveats

  • The recipe: hold vol-targeted TQQQ when Nasdaq > EMA-100 and the yield curve isn't inverted and the trend is clean (not choppy); de-lever to 1× QQQ when choppy; sit in long bonds when risk-off. Rebalance weekly.
  • Still leverage: −43% drawdowns will happen. This is a risk-managed leveraged strategy, not a safe one.
  • Overfitting guard: the winners are all economically motivated (bonds hedge, don't lever chop, curve leads recessions) and hold across all four eras — not a curve-fit parameter. The losers are reported honestly.
  • Data caveats: pre-2010 TQQQ/SQQQ simulated (conservative); bonds real from 2002 (T-bills before); credit spreads only 2007+; 2022 broke the stock-bond hedge (both fell). Costs modelled; taxes/slippage/borrow will lower live results.

Method — yfinance ^NDX/QQQ/TQQQ/SQQQ + macro (^TNX/^IRX/DX-Y.NYB/^VIX/TLT/GC=F); efficiency-ratio chop detector, EMA-100 trend, 25%-vol target, yield-curve (10y−3mo) & VIX & dollar overlays; weekly rebalance, weights shifted 1 day (no lookahead), 3 bps/turnover; ablation ladder + per-era stress.
