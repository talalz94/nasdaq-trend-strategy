On the 2016–26 bull, timing TQQQ looked like a cheat code. Stretch the test to 1985–2026 — including the dot-com crash and 2008 — and the picture inverts: buy-and-hold TQQQ (14.8% CAGR, −100% drawdown) doesn't even beat QQQ (14.9%). The naive 200-SMA timer that shone last decade gets −95% in 2000–09. What actually survives is vol-targeted leverage — and the best risk-adjusted answer needs no leverage at all.

04

What each knob taught

The grid swept signal × timeframe × rebalance frequency × allocation × vol-target × entry style. The signal (things people obsess over) mattered least; vol-targeting and the cash-vs-SQQQ choice mattered most.

Vol-targeting ★

The single biggest lever. Scaling TQQQ by 25%-target/realised-vol is what let leverage survive dot-com/GFC (−67% vs −95%).

Allocation

TQQQ/cash for max return; QQQ/cash for best Sharpe; SQQQ never — inverse LETFs decay to zero, cash is the correct defense.

Rebalance freq

Weekly ≈ daily, weekly slightly ahead (less whipsaw). Monthly lags. Checking less often doesn't hurt.

Signal

EMA ≈ SMA; EMA-100 best; crossovers didn't top the list. Length 100–250 all fine — robustness, not a magic number.

Signal timeframe

Daily ≈ weekly. A weekly-computed trend gives nearly the same result as daily — fewer signals, similar edge.

Entry style

Scale-in ≈ lump. Ramping in over 5 days barely changes returns; a small drawdown smoother, not a difference-maker.

05

The holistic answer

  • Want maximum growth & can stomach −67%? Vol-targeted trend-timed TQQQ: EMA-100 signal, rebalance weekly, 100% TQQQ when Nasdaq > EMA-100 else cash, scaled by 25%-vol target. CAGR ~32%, Calmar 0.47 — beats every buy-and-hold, survives dot-com.
  • Want the best risk-adjusted ride? Unleveraged QQQ/cash on the same EMA-100 weekly signal: Sharpe 0.91, ~QQQ return with half the drawdown (−43% vs −83%). No leverage, no heroics.
  • Never buy-and-hold a 3× ETF through a full cycle — dot-com would have wiped it out (−100%), and over 40 years it didn't even beat 1× QQQ.
  • Never hold SQQQ. Cash is the defensive leg.
  • Vol-target your leverage and rebalance weekly. Those two do more than any signal tweak.
06

Caveats

  • Pre-2010 TQQQ/SQQQ is simulated from Nasdaq-100 daily returns (0.95% expense + T-bill financing, daily-compounded), validated vs real 2010–26 (conservative, ~3.5%/yr tracking error). Accurate on crash mechanics; understates the 2010s trend bonus.
  • 1985–2026 was a historic tech bull. Don't extrapolate 30%+ CAGRs. Expect −45% to −67% leveraged drawdowns and long flat stretches.
  • Grid search on 40 years can still overfit. The robust conclusions are the ones that hold across all four eras (vol-target, cash-defense, weekly rebalance), not the single top row.
  • Costs modelled (3 bps/turnover); real slippage, borrow, funding and taxes will lower live results — most on the leveraged variants.
