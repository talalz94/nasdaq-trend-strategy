Why each rule exists

EMA-100 trend    leveraged ETFs decay in downtrends — don't own them there
Vol-target 25%   3× leverage in high vol is what digs the −80% craters; this is the #1 risk control
Efficiency ratio leverage bleeds sideways; drop to 1× in chop (cut max DD −67% → −47%)
Yield curve      the only leading macro signal that worked — inverts ~1yr before recessions
                 (VIX and the dollar failed: coincident signals sell you the bottom)
Bonds off-leg    treasuries rally in flight-to-safety — earns more than cash exactly when needed
