With leverage, timing
finally beats buy & hold.

Everywhere else in this study, timing a long-only signal just re-discovered market drift. Leverage breaks that. A dead-simple rule — hold 3× TQQQ while QQQ is above its 200-day average, sit in cash otherwise — beats TQQQ buy-and-hold on return and nearly halves the drawdown. The edge isn't predicting direction; it's dodging the −80% craters that leverage and volatility-decay dig.

03

Why it works — and why only with leverage

Drawdown avoidance compounds

A −82% drawdown needs a +456% gain just to recover; a −45% drawdown needs +82%. Cutting the deep losses lets the winners compound off a higher base. With 3× leverage the deep losses are frequent and savage, so avoiding them is worth more than the upside you miss sitting in cash ~25% of the time.

On plain QQQ, this barely helps

The same 200-SMA timer on unleveraged QQQ roughly matches buy-and-hold — consistent with the rest of the study: timing long-only exposure mostly captures the same beta. Leverage is what converts drawdown-avoidance into real outperformance.

It holds out-of-sample

Split at 2022 (TQQQ's −80% year): train 2016–21 the 200-SMA/cash timer made +1,125%; test 2022–26 it still made +161% (CAGR 23.5%) while riding through the crash. The rule isn't tuned to one era.

04

Read this before you lever up

  • Still a −45%+ drawdown. Better than −82%, but leverage is never tame — size for a halving.
  • 2016–26 was a historic Nasdaq bull. It flatters all Nasdaq exposure. In a flat or bear decade TQQQ buy-and-hold would be a disaster (−90%+) and the timer would win by even more — but absolute returns would be far lower. Don't extrapolate 39% CAGR forward.
  • 200-SMA whipsaws in chop. Choppy years produce false switches that bleed a little; the MA-Shift trend version had fewer whipsaws (Sharpe 1.01 vs 0.93).
  • Never hold SQQQ as a "hedge". Every short-leg variant lost — decay makes inverse leveraged ETFs a melting ice cube. Cash is the correct defensive position.
  • Data is 2016+ (Alpaca SIP floor), split-adjusted, dividends ignored (negligible for these ETFs).

Method — Alpaca SIP daily, split-adjusted, QQQ/TQQQ/SQQQ 2016-01-04→2026-07-24 (2,654 days). Signals on QQQ; position on day t set from close t−1; 2 bps per switch; benchmarked vs buy&hold QQQ and TQQQ; train/test split at 2022.
